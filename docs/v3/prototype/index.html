Maths · lesson 1 of 3 · suggested today

Equations as a balance

Analogy
Think of an equation as a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other so it stays level.
Idea
In 3x + 5 = 20, undo the +5 first, then undo the multiplication by 3. That gives 3x = 15 and x = 5.
Example
A box holds x pencils. Three boxes and five loose pencils make 20. Removing the five loose pencils leaves 15, so each box holds 5.
Visual · balance
Step 1 of 3 · remove 5 from both pans
Check
Solve 2x + 4 = 14.
Correct — Subtract 4 from both sides to get 2x = 10, then divide by 2.
